Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Manor on High located for couples searching for a Preston wedding venue?

We are at 519 High Street, Epping, in Melbourne’s northern suburbs, a 20-minute drive north of Preston. We offer three reception rooms with private courtyards and lounges at the one estate. You are welcome to arrange a visit and see how the location suits your ceremony and guest travel plans.

How do we choose the right reception room for our wedding?

We start with your estimated guest numbers and how you want to use the space. Our Regency Room welcomes 80 to 180 guests and our Georgian Room hosts up to 270 banquet guests. For larger weddings, we can talk through the Grand Ballroom and confirm a layout for dining, entertainment and dancing.

What do your all-inclusive wedding reception packages include?

Our packages cover venue hire, catering, décor, service staff and wedding coordination. If you are comparing all-inclusive reception venues, we can explain the inclusions, customisation options and any costs for requested upgrades. We will discuss your guest numbers and preferences to prepare a quote for your celebration.

Can we personalise the wedding menu and décor?

Yes, we offer customisable menus and décor so your celebration reflects your tastes. We will talk through your preferred food, colours, styling and any dietary requirements. Your wedding coordinator can explain the available options and how your choices fit within your selected package.

Is there parking for guests travelling from Preston?

Yes, we provide complimentary parking for guests who drive. When planning the trip from Preston, allow for traffic, your ceremony location and any photography stops. If you are arranging group transport, let us know your arrival and departure times so we can build them into your reception schedule.
